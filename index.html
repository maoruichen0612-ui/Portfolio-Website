About me01 / 02

Computer science meets
creative curiosity.

I’m Ray, a computer science student with a growing passion for website design. I enjoy combining logical problem-solving with visual creativity to make digital experiences that feel clear, engaging, and easy to use.

A lot of my hands-on learning happens through Minecraft MakeCode, where I design and build mini-games. These experiments let me practice programming fundamentals, game logic, level design, testing, and creative problem-solving in a playful environment. Every mini-game teaches me something new about how people interact with the things I create.

I’m now bringing that same curiosity to the web—learning HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive design, and user interface principles one project at a time. This portfolio will grow alongside me as I turn ideas into finished work and document what I learn along the way.

I’m interested in opportunities at leading technology and design companies where I can learn from experienced teams, contribute a fresh perspective, and continue developing as both a programmer and a designer.
